Happy Birthday to someone who means more to me than words can easily put on a screen. Looking at all these moments together reminds me just how much light, strength, and character you bring into the spaces around you. Growing up and sharing life with you has taught me so much—not just about family, but about resilience, warmth, and showing up even when things aren't easy.

I really admire the way you carry yourself and the heart you have for the people you care about. Even when life gets busy, noisy, or unpredictable, there is something steady about you that people naturally gravitate toward. You have a way of making ordinary days feel a little brighter, whether through a random laugh, a real conversation, or simply being there when it counts.

As you step into this new year, my biggest wish for you is peace of mind, genuine joy, and doors opening in ways that surprise you for the better. I hope you give yourself permission to celebrate how far you’ve come, to rest without guilt, and to chase the things that truly make you feel alive and fulfilled. May every step you take this year bring you closer to the goals and peace you’ve been working toward.

No matter how much time passes or how busy life gets, you always have someone in your corner cheering for you, rooting for your wins, and wishing you nothing but the absolute best. Thank you for being you, and thank you for being my sister. Have the happiest birthday—today is all yours.

With all my love
